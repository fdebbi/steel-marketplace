## Article 2 — Purpose

These terms set the rules for using ReuSteel, the Algerian steel platform: market for new products, reuse steel and steel buildings, requests for quotation, fabrication and site services, engineering studies and inspections, tools (weight calculator, cutting optimiser, catalogue of sections) and AI assistant. Signing up means accepting these terms and the [Privacy policy](page:privacy).

## Article 3 — Sign-up and accounts

- Sign-up is free and reserved for persons **aged 18 or over** and for businesses represented by an authorised person.
- Three types of account: **company**, **professional** (craftsman or self-employed) and **client**. Partner engineers sign up through the [Become a partner engineer](page:engineer-apply) page and are accepted after their diploma and approval have been checked.
- Users give accurate information and keep it up to date. They confirm their contact with a 6-digit code before publishing a listing or a request for quotation.
- A company can send its documents (RC, NIF, NIS) to obtain the **verified business** badge. This badge means that the ReuSteel team has checked these documents; it guarantees neither the quality of the products nor the performance of sales.
- The account is personal. Users keep their password secret and are responsible for actions taken with their account.

## Article 4 — Role of ReuSteel

- For listings, requests for quotation, offers and orders between users, ReuSteel is a **technical intermediary**: it puts buyers and sellers in touch. It does not sell the steel published by users, does not own it and is not a party to the contracts they conclude (price, delivery, payment, warranties).
- For **engineering**, ReuSteel is the client's point of contact: it sets the price, chooses a verified partner engineer and commits to the quote and the deadline. The client and the engineer do not see each other and do not communicate directly.
- ReuSteel publishes no market prices or steel prices. The prices shown are those asked by each seller.

## Article 5 — Listings

- Any account, except partner engineers, can publish listings, within the limits of its plan.
- The seller guarantees that they have the right to sell what they publish and that the listing is accurate: designation, grade, dimensions, quantity, price, real photos of the batch.
- For reuse steel, the **condition** (new surplus, used in excellent or good condition, requires inspection or treatment, scrap) is the **seller's declaration**, under their responsibility.
- Forbidden: false, misleading or duplicate listings, goods of illegal origin or stolen goods, contact details (phone, e-mail, address) written in the text or photos, and any content contrary to Algerian law.
- The ReuSteel team can edit, suspend or remove a listing that breaks these rules; the reason is sent to the seller by e-mail.

## Article 6 — Contacts, offers and orders

- No purchase, offer or message without an account.
- Users' phone numbers and e-mails are never shown publicly. They are given to the other party **after acceptance** of an order or an offer.
- Sale, delivery and payment are settled directly between buyer and seller, who are solely responsible for them. Buyers are advised to check the batch before committing.

## Article 7 — Requests for quotation

- The requester describes their need; companies and professionals answer with an offer (price, lead time, terms).
- The requester sees all offers; each supplier sees only their own; visitors see none.
- An offer binds its author until the date given. When the requester accepts an offer, the other suppliers are notified.

## Article 8 — Engineering

- Every request for a study or inspection goes through the platform. ReuSteel sends a quote with a delivery date; the mission starts once the client accepts the quote.
- “Engineer verified on ReuSteel” means the team has checked their diploma and approval; it is not an official certification.
- The partner engineer undertakes to meet the mission deadlines, keep the file confidential and not contact the client outside the platform.

## Article 9 — Tools and AI assistant

- The calculator, the catalogue and the optimiser give **theoretical weights** calculated from standard dimensions; the actual weight may vary with manufacturing tolerances.
- The AI assistant makes **suggestions** (listing text, recognition of a section, condition estimated from photos, reading a document). Nothing is applied without the user's click, and the user checks the result. An estimate from photos never replaces an inspection.
- These tools do not replace an engineer's opinion on a structure.

## Article 10 — Plans, credits and payment

- Some features depend on the chosen plan (number of listings, warehouse, AI credits, Business Report). Plan and credit prices are shown on the [Plans](page:pricing) page.
- Payment is made offline (bank transfer, CCP, BaridiMob): the user sends proof of payment, the team activates the plan and an invoice is issued.
- AI credits that have been used are not refundable, except in the event of a technical error by the platform.
