## Article 2 — Data collected

- **Account**: name, phone, e-mail, password (stored encrypted, never readable), language, type of account.
- **Company or professional**: company name, trade, wilaya and commune, map pin placed by the user, logo; for the verified badge: numbers and copies of the RC, NIF and NIS.
- **Partner engineer**: diploma, identity document, approval, photo, specialties, availability.
- **Activity on the site**: listings and photos, requests for quotation, offers, orders, messages, study requests and attached documents, plan, proofs of payment and invoices.
- **AI tools**: the photos, texts or documents the user sends to the assistant, only when they use it.
- **Contact form**: name, e-mail, message.
- **Technical log**: sign-in dates and important actions (security and proof). Catalogue searches are counted without any link to the person.

## Article 3 — Why we use this data (purposes)

- Create and manage the account, verify the contact (6-digit code) and, on request, the company or the engineer.
- Publish listings, pass on messages, offers and orders, then give contact details to the other party after acceptance.
- Handle study and inspection requests with a partner engineer.
- Manage plans, payments and invoices.
- Send e-mails related to the account (codes, notifications, reminders) in the user's language.
- Keep the site secure and prevent fraud.
- Meet our legal obligations (invoicing, requests from the authorities).

This processing is based on the **consent** given at sign-up, on the **performance of the contract** (the terms of use) and on our **legal obligations**. ReuSteel sends no advertising by e-mail without agreement and **sells no data**.

## Article 4 — Who sees your data

- **The public**: the company name, its wilaya, its logo, the “verified business” badge and the listings. **Never** the phone, the e-mail, the verification documents or the exact address.
- **The other party to a sale**: your name, phone and e-mail, only after an order or an offer has been accepted.
- **Suppliers answering your request for quotation**: the request, without your contact details before acceptance.
- **The partner engineer**: the mission file (structure, wilaya, documents), **without** the client's contact details; the client does not see the engineer's either.
- **The ReuSteel team**: what is needed for verification, moderation and support.
- **Our technical providers**, acting only on our instructions: the website host ({host}), the e-mail sending service, the AI assistant provider (only for what is sent to the assistant) and, once active, the SMS sending service. The site's maps are displayed from OpenStreetMap, which receives the visitor's IP address.
- **The authorities**, upon lawful request.

## Article 5 — Data sent outside Algeria

The website host and the AI assistant provider are located outside Algeria. This transfer takes place under the conditions laid down by Law 18-07{transfer}.

## Article 6 — Security

Encrypted connection (HTTPS); passwords stored encrypted; RC, NIF and NIS numbers encrypted; private documents kept outside the public part of the site and opened only through personal, time-limited links; protection against robots and repeated attempts; team access limited to what is necessary. In the event of a data breach, ReuSteel informs the ANPDP and the persons concerned as the law provides.

## Article 7 — Retention period

| Data | Period |
| --- | --- |
| Account and profile | As long as the account is open; deleted or anonymised within 30 days after closure |
| Verification documents (RC, NIF, NIS, diploma, identity, approval) | As long as the account is open; deleted within 30 days after closure or after a refusal |
| Listings | As long as they are online; removed when the account is closed |
| Messages, offers, orders, requests for quotation and studies | 3 years after the last related activity, as proof in the event of a dispute |
| Invoices and proofs of payment | 10 years (accounting and tax obligations) |
| Contact form | 1 year after the reply |
| Technical log (security) | 1 year |
| Account without sign-in for 3 years | Notified by e-mail, then closed one month later without a reply |
| Language cookie | 1 year |

## Article 8 — Your rights

You have the right:

- to be **informed** of how your data is used (this page);
- to **access** your data and obtain a copy;
- to have it **corrected, updated or erased**; ReuSteel answers within **10 days**;
- to **object** to processing on legitimate grounds, and to any marketing message;
- to **withdraw your consent** at any time (this leads to the closure of the account, except for data the law requires us to keep).

To exercise these rights, write to {dpo_email} from your account's address. Most profile information can also be changed directly in *My account*. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can refer the matter to the **ANPDP**.

## Article 9 — Cookies

ReuSteel uses **no advertising or audience measurement cookies**. Only technical cookies are set: the chosen language (1 year), the account sign-in and a marker telling the site that a user is signed in (length of the session). They are necessary for the site to work.

## Article 10 — Minors

The site is reserved for persons aged **18 or over**. ReuSteel does not knowingly collect data from minors; an account opened by a minor is closed as soon as it is reported.

The new version is published on this page with its date; any significant change is announced by e-mail.
