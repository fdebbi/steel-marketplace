# Buying reused steel: what to check

Before buying reused steel, check five things: the declared condition, how the weight was obtained, the photos and the origin, the lot itself on site, and an engineer's opinion if the steel will carry loads.

On ReuSteel, reuse covers offcuts of sections and bars, sections from dismantling, tubes, plate offcuts, mixed lots and scrap. This steel can cost less than new and avoids throwing away a material that is still useful. But every lot is different: this guide explains how to read a listing and what to check before you pay.

## 1. Read the declared condition

Every reuse listing must state a condition, chosen by the seller on a five-level scale. It is the first filter: it tells you what the lot can be used for.

| Condition on ReuSteel | What it means | Possible use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unused new surplus | Steel never installed, left over from a site or from stock | Like new, after a visual check |
| Used – excellent condition | Already used, few or no visible defects | Reuse after verification |
| Used – good condition | Already used, minor defects (surface rust, holes, marks) | Reuse after verification and treatment if needed |
| To inspect or treat | Uncertain condition or defects to assess | Inspection required before any use |
| Scrap / recycling only | Steel bound for melting | Recycling only, never in a structure |

The condition is **declared by the seller**: ReuSteel does not certify it. It helps you sort listings; it does not replace your own check. For the "To inspect" condition, the listing page shows a stronger warning.

## 2. Weighed or calculated weight

A price per kilo is only worth something if the weight is right. On ReuSteel, the listing shows how the weight was obtained.

- **Sections, bars and tubes**: the seller enters an inventory line by line (type, section, number of pieces, length). The site calculates the weight of each line and of the lot from the European sections catalogue. This is a **theoretical weight**. The seller can also give the weight they measured on a scale.
- **Plates, mixed lots and scrap**: the lot is sold by weight, **weighed or estimated**. The listing shows which.

A theoretical or estimated weight is not a weighed one. Approximate lengths, drilled pieces or metal lost to rust change the real weight. For a large lot, ask for a weighbridge ticket and agree with the seller on the weight the invoice will use.

To check the weight of a line yourself, use the ReuSteel weight calculator: choose the section, the length and the number of pieces.

## 3. Photos, origin and lot details

The other information in the listing completes the condition and the weight. Read all of it before contacting the seller.

- **Photos**: look at the bar ends, the webs and flanges, the welded areas. Flaking rust, bent pieces or notches should be visible. If a view is missing, ask the seller for it.
- **Origin** (required): site surplus, workshop offcuts, dismantled structure, end of stock or other. A section from a dismantled structure has already carried loads; site surplus has not.
- **Surface condition**: clean, surface rust, painted, galvanised or heavy corrosion. It changes the preparation work (blasting, painting) and so the total cost.
- **What the price includes**: loading on site, transport, cutting to order (bars and sections only). Without loading, plan the lifting equipment; without transport, the truck.

## 4. See the lot before paying

Nothing is paid on ReuSteel: payment and collection are settled directly between you and the seller. So go and see the lot before paying, especially for a large quantity.

On site, with a tape measure and a caliper:

1. **Check the section**: measure the height, width and thickness. Compare with the section listed in the ReuSteel sections catalogue.
2. **Count and measure** the pieces: the number and lengths must match the listing's inventory.
3. **Check straightness**: a bent or twisted bar is hard to reuse.
4. **Look for defects**: deep corrosion, cracks, holes, welds, torch cuts.
5. **Have it weighed** if the price depends on weight and the listed weight was not weighed.

Write down what you agreed with the seller (quantity, weight, price, collection) before paying.

## 5. Reuse in a structure: an engineer's opinion

Used steel should carry a load (beam, column, roof frame) only after a qualified engineer has checked it. That is why every reuse listing on ReuSteel shows this notice.

The engineer checks in particular:

- that the steel grade and the section suit your project;
- the real condition of the pieces (corrosion, deformation, holes, old welds);
- the planned connections;
- the structural calculation for your site.

On ReuSteel, you can request an inspection or a study from the **Engineering** page: the platform sends you a quote, then assigns the job to an engineer verified on ReuSteel.

For a non-load-bearing use (fence, light shelter, workshop shelving), a careful visual check is often enough. Scrap goes to recycling only.

## 6. Buying or making an offer on ReuSteel

You need to be signed in to buy or make an offer. From the listing page:

1. **Choose the quantity**, as the seller allows: the whole lot at the lot price, whole bars, kilos (up to the listed weight) or pieces.
2. **Buy at the displayed price or make an offer** with the total price you propose.
3. **The seller replies**: they accept, decline or propose another price. You can in turn accept, decline or reply. There are at most 3 replies with a price; without a reply, the request closes after 7 days.
4. **Once the deal is accepted**, you receive the seller's phone number and email, and the quantity is removed from the listing.
5. **You arrange together** the visit, payment, loading and transport.

You follow each request in *My account → My purchases and offers*. Can't find what you need? Post a **request for quotation**: suppliers reply with their offers.

## Checklist

- [ ] The declared condition suits the intended use
- [ ] I know whether the weight is weighed, calculated or estimated
- [ ] The photos show the ends and the defects
- [ ] The origin and surface condition are stated
- [ ] I know what the price includes (loading, transport, cutting)
- [ ] I have seen the lot: section, number, lengths, straightness
- [ ] The weight used for the price is agreed in writing
- [ ] For a load-bearing use, an engineer has checked the pieces
