# Less waste: ordering 6 m and 12 m bars the right way

Offcuts come from a poor match between the length of the pieces and the length of the bars you buy. Before ordering, have your pieces laid out on 6 m bars and on 12 m bars: for the same list, the difference can be several bars.

Each cut also uses up a little steel (the saw kerf). A bar holding n pieces needs n − 1 saw cuts between them, and one last cut to free the offcut.

## 1. An example: 6 pieces of 3.90 m

For 6 pieces of 3.90 m in the same section, with a 3 mm saw kerf, 12 m bars need 12 m less steel than 6 m bars.

| Bars bought | Number of bars | Length bought | Pieces per bar | Offcut per bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 m | 6 | 36 m | 1 | about 2.10 m |
| 12 m | 2 | 24 m | 3 | about 0.29 m |

With 6 m bars, each bar holds only one piece and leaves a 2.10 m offcut. With 12 m bars, three pieces fit on one bar.

The opposite also happens: depending on the lengths, 6 m bars can give the better result, and they are easier to transport. There is no general rule: you have to calculate your list.

## 2. Using the cutting optimiser

The ReuSteel cutting optimiser is free and open to everyone, without an account. The calculation is exact (it does not use AI): it looks for the smallest number of new bars.

1. **Enter your pieces**: for each line, the section, the length of one piece and the number (up to 40 lines and 300 pieces per section).
2. **Choose the bar length**: 6 m, 12 m or another length.
3. **Set the saw kerf** (3 mm by default) and the length from which an offcut is reusable (1 m by default).
4. **Add your offcuts in stock**, if you have any: they are used first.
5. **Read the result**: bars to buy, cutting plan bar by bar, offcuts in metres, kilos and per cent, reusable offcuts.
6. **Keep the plan**: print, PDF or a link to share with the workshop.
7. **Request a quote** for the bars to buy: the request is pre-filled (account needed).

Compare two calculations, one with 6 m bars and one with 12 m, before ordering. The weights are theoretical, taken from the catalogue, and the plan is an indicative calculation to check before cutting.

## 3. Starting from a bill of quantities

If your list is already in a document, the **Read a document (AI bill of quantities)** tool turns it into a list of pieces for the optimiser. You need an account, and each reading uses one AI credit.

- Accepted documents: bill of quantities (DQE), quote, purchase order, take-off, contract… as Excel (.xlsx), CSV, PDF or photo. Drawings are not read.
- Each line read is marked **"sure"** if everything is written in the document, or **"to check"** if the section is unknown, the length or number is missing, the line was inferred, or the source is a photo.
- Nothing is added without you: you tick the lines to keep.
- A line given in metres or by weight gets 12 m bars (editable); the number is calculated and marked "to check".
- Plates and base plates go to the quote request, with their weight.

Always review the "to check" lines before running the calculation.

## 4. Reuse before you buy

The optimiser can look for steel in this order, and each step only receives the pieces the previous one did not place:

1. **Your offcuts and your warehouse**: the offcuts you enter, and your company's warehouse if you use it on ReuSteel. A bar already started is filled before another one is taken.
2. **The ReuSteel market**: published bars of the same section, of equal length or the nearest above, then the cheapest listing per kilo. You choose the minimum steel grade, the accepted conditions (never scrap), the wilaya, and whether lots sold whole are allowed. Your own listings are not offered.
3. **New bars** for the rest.

For each market listing, the result shows the bars taken, the pieces cut, the weight and an estimated cost from the displayed price. It is a snapshot of the market at the time of the calculation: nothing is reserved, you then need to buy or make an offer.

After cutting, the reusable offcuts are listed with their length: put them in the warehouse for the next job, or publish them as a reuse listing.
