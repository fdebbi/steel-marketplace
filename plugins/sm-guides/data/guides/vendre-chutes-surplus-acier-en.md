# Selling steel offcuts and surplus

Your offcuts, site surplus and end-of-stock steel can be sold on ReuSteel, in the **Reuse** market. A clear listing — the right category, the real condition, an explained weight, good photos — sells faster and avoids arguments at collection.

Any ReuSteel account can publish a reuse listing, except partner engineer accounts.

## 1. Choosing the right category

The category decides how the lot is described: line by line, or by weight.

| Category | How the lot is described |
| --- | --- |
| Section and bar offcuts | Inventory line by line: type, section, number of pieces, length |
| Sections from dismantling | Inventory line by line |
| Reuse tubes | Inventory line by line |
| Plate offcuts | Lot by weight, thickness required |
| Mixed lots | Lot by weight |
| Scrap (heavy, light, mixed) | Lot by weight |

For an inventory, the site calculates the weight of each line and of the lot from the sections catalogue. You do not have to calculate it yourself. If you do not recognise a section, the photo recognition tool in the form suggests the possible sections: you make the choice.

## 2. Describing the lot

The buyer judges your lot on what they read and see. Give them the information they will check on site anyway.

- **Condition** (required): unused new surplus, used – excellent condition, used – good condition, to inspect or treat, scrap / recycling only. Choose the real condition, not the best one.
- **Origin** (required): site surplus, workshop offcuts, dismantled structure, end of stock or other.
- **Surface condition**: clean, surface rust, painted, galvanised or heavy corrosion.
- **Weight**: for an inventory, the calculated weight is theoretical; if you weighed the lot, enter the weighed weight. For a lot sold by weight, say whether it is weighed or estimated: the buyer sees the difference.
- **What you offer**: loading on site, transport, cutting to order.
- **Photos**: overall view, bar ends, markings, defects. A defect shown avoids an argument at collection.

The **Write with AI** button can write the description from what you entered, without inventing anything. You review it, edit it or cancel it.

## 3. Setting the price

You choose the price unit: **per kilo**, **per piece** or **for the whole lot**. For a lot price, the site also shows the equivalent per kilo, so the buyer can compare.

To find a fair price:

- look at similar listings on ReuSteel: the pages by type and by wilaya show the range of prices asked by sellers, from 3 listings with a price;
- take into account the condition, the surface and what you include (loading, transport, cutting);
- a price per kilo depends on a weight: say clearly whether it is weighed or calculated.

These ranges are asking prices, not sale prices. The buyer can also make you an offer: you are free to accept, decline or propose another price.

## 4. Platform rules

The form applies these rules automatically:

- condition and origin are required;
- scrap is published only in the Scrap category, with the condition "Scrap / recycling only";
- cutting to order applies only to bars and sections.

The information in the listing is declared by you and is not certified by ReuSteel. Every reuse listing reminds the buyer that used steel should be reused in a structure only after an engineer has checked it.

The ReuSteel team can remove a listing that does not follow the rules; you are sent the reason.

## 5. After publishing

Requests arrive in *My account → Orders and offers received*, with a counter of replies waiting. You are notified on the site and by email.

1. **A buyer buys or makes an offer** on the whole lot or part of it, as you allowed. You receive their name, phone number and email.
2. **You reply**: accept, decline or propose another price. There are at most 3 replies with a price; without a reply, the request closes after 7 days.
3. **When you accept**, the quantity is removed from the listing. At zero, the listing becomes "Sold". The buyer receives your contact details.
4. **You settle together** payment, the visit and collection: nothing is paid on ReuSteel.

Remember to update the listing if part of the lot is sold elsewhere, so you do not sell the same thing twice.

## Checklist (seller)

- [ ] I chose the right category (inventory or lot by weight)
- [ ] The condition chosen is the real condition
- [ ] The origin and surface condition are stated
- [ ] I said whether the weight is weighed, calculated or estimated
- [ ] The photos show the overall view, the ends and the defects
- [ ] I stated what I include (loading, transport, cutting)
- [ ] My price and its unit are clear (kilo, piece or lot)
- [ ] I reply to requests within 7 days
- [ ] I update the listing if part of it is sold elsewhere
