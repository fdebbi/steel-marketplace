## Account

### Who can sign up on ReuSteel?

Anyone can sign up for free, with a **company** account (supplier, manufacturer, construction company, recycler, dismantler…), a **professional** account (craftsman or self-employed, chosen by trade) or a **client** account (to buy and request quotes). Engineers go through a separate page: [Become a partner engineer](page:engineer-apply).

### What is the difference between the accounts?

- **Client**: buy, publish listings, request a quote, fabrication or a study.
- **Company and professional**: all of that, plus answering requests for quotation. Manufacturers, construction companies and trades also offer their fabrication and erection services.
- **Partner engineer**: carries out the studies and inspections the platform assigns. Partner engineers do not publish listings or answer requests for quotation.

### Why do I have to confirm my contact with a code?

Before publishing a listing or a request for quotation, you confirm your contact with a 6-digit code sent by SMS or by e-mail. This keeps out fake accounts. A company can then send its documents (RC, NIF, NIS) to get the **verified business** badge. Only the company itself and the ReuSteel team can see these documents.

## Publishing a listing

### Who can publish a listing?

Any ReuSteel account can publish new products, reuse steel or a steel building, except partner engineer accounts. You only need to have confirmed your contact. The number of listings depends on your plan.

### How do I declare the condition of reuse steel?

You choose one of the 5 levels: *New unused surplus*, *Excellent used condition*, *Good used condition*, *Requires inspection or treatment*, *Scrap / recycling only*. The condition is your own declaration: the AI assistant can suggest a level from the photos, but nothing is applied without your click, and an estimate from photos never replaces an inspection. See the guide [Buying reuse steel](guide:acheter-acier-reemploi).

### Why is my listing not showing yet?

Open *My account → My listings* and check its status:

- **Draft**: a required detail is missing, or you withdrew it.
- **Waiting for review**: the ReuSteel team is checking it before publication.
- **Expired** or **paused**: republish it with one click from the same page.
- **Rejected**: the reason was sent to you by e-mail; correct it, then republish it.

### How do I sell my offcuts and surplus?

Publish them on the reuse market, with the grade, the dimensions and the condition. The weight is calculated from the catalogue. The guide [Selling steel offcuts and surplus](guide:vendre-chutes-surplus-acier) explains how to prepare a clear listing.

## Buying and requesting a quote

### How do I contact a seller?

You need an account: without one, you cannot buy, make an offer or send a message. The **Get in touch** button on the listing opens a message window. The seller's phone and e-mail are never shown publicly: you receive them when your order or your offer is accepted.

### How do I request a quote?

In *My account → My requests for quotation*, describe what you need: sections from the catalogue (the weight is calculated automatically) or a free description, delivery wilaya, desired date, deadline for offers, and drawings if needed. Suppliers and manufacturers answer with their price, lead time and terms. You can also create a request directly from the cutting optimiser.

### Who sees the offers I receive?

Only you see all the offers, side by side, sorted by total, price per kilo, lead time or verified suppliers. Each supplier sees only their own offer, and visitors see none. When you accept an offer, the other suppliers are notified automatically.

### Why are prices in DA/kg?

Steel is sold by weight. A price per kilo lets you compare listings of different sections and lengths. ReuSteel calculates the weight from the catalogue dimensions, then the total. A seller can also give a total price in an offer.

### How do I check a batch before buying?

Look at the declared condition, the photos and the **verified business** badge, then ask your questions by message before you commit. For a large batch or a steel frame, you can request an inspection by an engineer through the platform. The guide [Buying reuse steel](guide:acheter-acier-reemploi) lists the points to check.

## Engineering

### How do I request a study or an inspection?

Fill in the [Request an engineering study](page:mission-request) form: type of mission, structure, wilaya, documents. Every request goes through the platform. The ReuSteel team sets the price, chooses a suitable verified engineer and sends you a quote with the delivery date. You then follow the progress from your account.

### Why can't I see the engineer's name or contact details?

This is intentional. ReuSteel is your only point of contact: the platform commits to the price, the deadline and the follow-up, and the engineer works on the file without direct contact. In the same way, the engineer does not see your contact details.

### What does “engineer verified on ReuSteel” mean?

The ReuSteel team checked the engineer's diploma and approval before assigning missions. It is not an official certification.

### I am an engineer: how do I become a partner?

Go to the [Become a partner engineer](page:engineer-apply) page and send your diploma and approval. Once verified, you receive missions with their deadlines, and reminders before each due date.

## Tools

### What is the weight calculator for?

It gives the theoretical weight of a section, tube or plate from its designation (IPE, HEA, HEB, UPN, angles…) and its length or dimensions. It is free and helps you estimate transport, compare prices per kilo or prepare a request for quotation. The actual weight may vary slightly with manufacturing tolerances.

### What does the cutting optimiser do?

You enter the pieces to cut. The optimiser spreads them over 6 m or 12 m bars to reduce offcuts, looks first in your warehouse and then on the market, and gives you a cutting plan as a PDF. It can also create the request for quotation directly. See the guide [Reducing offcuts from 6 and 12 m bars](guide:reduire-chutes-barres-6-12-m).

### Where do the catalogue dimensions come from?

The [catalogue of sections](page:catalogue) gathers the common European sections with their dimensions and weight per metre, checked by the ReuSteel team. The calculator, the optimiser and the listings all use the same data.

## Languages and trust

### Is the site available in Arabic and English?

Yes. ReuSteel is in French, Arabic and English. Your choice is remembered, and e-mails reach you in your language. A listing can be written in French and Arabic; technical designations (IPE 200, S275JR) and units stay the same everywhere.

### Are my contact details public?

No. No phone number or e-mail is shown on the site. Your contact details are given to the other party only after an order or an offer is accepted. Documents sent for verification are visible only to you and the ReuSteel team.

### Does ReuSteel publish market prices?

No. The prices shown are those each seller asks in their listing. ReuSteel publishes no steel prices and no price statistics on its public pages.

### How do I contact the ReuSteel team?

Through the form on the [Contact](page:contact) page or at contact@reusteel.com.
